macOS.
On your PC.

The real Apple-silicon macOS on desktops and laptops, straight from a USB stick.

It starts with a USB stick. Plug it into any PC.

Open the lid. Turn it on.

Stambha starts first. It recreates the Apple hardware macOS expects.

Then macOS. The real one, made for Apple silicon.

No Mac. No Hackintosh. No virtual machine.

Scroll

Stambha translates macOS's Arm code to your PC's processor as it runs, drives your NVIDIA graphics card with its own Metal driver, and recreates the Apple hardware macOS expects. All from one USB stick.
