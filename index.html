Already working

Stambha translates macOS's Arm code to x86 as it runs, drives your NVIDIA card through its own Metal driver, and recreates the Apple hardware macOS expects. Here is what that gets you today.

  • macOS on your PC

    Apple-silicon macOS boots with real GPU acceleration on NVIDIA GTX 16 and RTX 20, 30, 40 and 50 cards.

  • Install over Wi-Fi

    No cable needed with a Realtek RTL8852CE, the Wi-Fi 6E chip in many recent laptops. Ethernet works too.

  • A careful installer

    It only ever uses empty space on your disk, and checks every write before the next one.

  • Progress you can see

    A line every minute with the stage, percent and speed, so a long install never looks frozen.

Your other systems stay put

Installing macOS next to Windows or Linux should never put them at risk. Make some empty space on your disk first, and Stambha installs into that space and nowhere else.

  • Never resizes, moves or deletes a partition. It uses only space you left unallocated.
  • Checks both copies of your partition table and refuses a layout it doesn't expect.
  • Reads back every write before it makes the next one.
  • Check only runs the whole install plan as a dry run and writes nothing at all.

Coming in the first release

Today Stambha is slower than a real Mac. The first release is mostly about changing that, and about making laptops feel at home.

  • Speed

    Apple's matrix unit (AMX) running natively on your CPU, faster translation, and faster installs.

  • Wi-Fi in macOS

    Your Wi-Fi inside macOS, plus a network picker in the boot menu that remembers your saved networks.

  • Laptops

    Battery level and charging, a touchpad that feels like a Magic Trackpad, brightness and keyboard-light keys, lid and power button, audio fixes.

  • Bluetooth

    Realtek Bluetooth, for your mouse, keyboard and headphones.

After that: AMD and Intel graphics, more Wi-Fi chips, and laptops that show up as a MacBook. Follow along in the roadmap channel on Discord.

What you need

Most gaming desktops and laptops from the last five years or so fit. Not sure about yours? Ask in the help channel and someone will check.

GraphicsNVIDIA GeForce GTX 16 or RTX 20, 30, 40, 50 series. On laptops with a MUX switch, choose dGPU-only mode.
ProcessorAny Intel or AMD x86-64 chip from Intel Nehalem or AMD Bulldozer onward. AVX2 and AVX-512 are used when present.
Memory8 GB or more. 16 GB is better.
DiskNVMe, or SATA in AHCI mode, with 60 GB or more of empty, unallocated space.
NetworkEthernet (Intel, Realtek, Broadcom, Aquantia, Qualcomm Atheros and common USB adapters) or Realtek RTL8852CE Wi-Fi.
Firmware64-bit UEFI with Secure Boot turned off.
USB stick8 GB or more.
